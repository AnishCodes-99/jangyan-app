# JanGyan — National Citizen Awareness & E-Learning Platform

**Official Tagline:** *Learn Without Limits*  
**Project Type:** Interactive Citizen Literacy & Assessment Web Portal  
**Target Audience:** Indian Citizens, Students, Evaluators & Academic Reviewers

---

## 🌟 Overview
**JanGyan** is an e-learning and civic literacy platform created to bridge the citizen awareness gap in India. It organizes essential everyday knowledge across 6 critical domains into modular topics and provides an interactive 20-MCQ assessment engine with real-time feedback and downloadable certificates of awareness.

---

## 🏛️ The 6 Core Domains
1. **Cyber Safety & Digital Security** — Phishing prevention, OTP & 2FA protection, National Cyber Crime Helpline **1930**, DPDP Act 2023.
2. **Government Services & Citizen Rights** — Constitution of India (Article 1, Part III), Right to Information (RTI Act 2005), DigiLocker validity, CPGRAMS grievance portal.
3. **Road Safety & Public Safety** — Motor Vehicles Act 2019, Good Samaritan Protection (Section 134A), Golden Hour protocols, Cashless Treatment Scheme 2025.
4. **Health & Emergency Awareness** — Unified Emergency Number **112**, CPR chest compressions (30:2 ratio), Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (₹5 Lakh cover), Silent NCD diseases.
5. **Financial Literacy & Digital Finance** — Safe UPI & QR rules, 50-30-20 budgeting rule, emergency funds, CIBIL scores, Ponzi scheme detection, NPS & APY pensions.
6. **Environment & Civic Responsibility** — Three-bin solid waste segregation (Wet/Dry/Hazardous), single-use plastic ban, Air Quality Index (AQI), Article 51A(g) civic duties.

---

## 🚀 Key Features for College Evaluation
- **Official Question Bank:** 150+ authentic questions extracted from JanGyan handbooks (25 curated questions per category).
- **Customizable Assessment Engine:**
  - Standard 20-MCQ evaluation mode ("solve 20-20 MCQs each time").
  - Dynamic randomization algorithm so every quiz attempt is unique.
  - Quick Practice (10 questions) & Full Challenge (25 questions) options.
  - Mixed Master Challenge across all 6 domains.
- **Academic Feedback & Explanations:**
  - Instant visual feedback (Green / Red indicator).
  - Official JanGyan Handbook explanation card after each question.
  - Question Navigator (1 to 20) with live status tracking.
- **Results Dashboard & Analytics:**
  - Final score, accuracy percentage, time taken telemetry.
  - Civic proficiency badge (*Citizen Champion*, *Civic Scholar*, *Aware Citizen*).
  - Full question review accordion with user's answer vs correct answer and rationale.
- **Printable JanGyan Citizen Certificate:**
  - User can enter their name to generate a government-style, verified e-Certificate.
  - Print / Save as PDF support with print-optimized CSS layout.
- **National Emergency Directory & Portals:**
  - One-click access to verified helplines: **112**, **1930**, **108**, **1098**, **1091**, **14444**.
  - Direct links to official portals: *cybercrime.gov.in*, *parivahan.gov.in*, *pgportal.gov.in*, *digilocker.gov.in*.
- **Interactive UI Polish:**
  - Dark / Light mode toggle with local storage persistence.
  - Audio synthesizer for correct/wrong feedback (toggleable).
  - Responsive layout for desktop, tablets, and smartphones.
  - 100% standalone offline support (no backend required; double-click `index.html` to run).
